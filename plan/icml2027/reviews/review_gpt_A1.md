**1. ICML review today**

*Summary.* The paper evaluates a deployed auditing configuration across general code, scientific code, scientific results and tabular data. Repeated auditing increases both coverage and false alarms; instructions and decision rules substantially change reported recall. Model ratings associate persistent misses with specifications that do not entail hidden-test expectations. Small scientific studies illustrate complementary detections by model readers and deterministic checks.

| Criterion | Score / 10 | Assessment |
|---|---:|---|
| Soundness | **5** | Headline counts reproduce; construct validity and causal identification remain weak. |
| Presentation | **4** | Careful qualifications, but an overloaded argument and extensive procedural narration. |
| Significance | **4** | Important problem; limited evidence of a transferable finding or useful improvement. |
| Originality | **4** | Most observations are established principles; the specification mechanism could become distinctive. |
| Overall | **4** | **Reject**, with a plausible route to a substantially stronger submission. |

The strengths are real:

- Naturally occurring generator failures, executable benchmark outcomes and test-passing controls are a useful foundation.
- The archived records, explicit departures and reporting of unsuccessful hypotheses support unusually detailed scrutiny.
- The decision-rule reversal is instructive: “better auditor” rankings depend on what counts as a finding.
- The science studies contain useful examples of evaluation defects and complementary detection. The held-out experiment is a meaningful improvement over retrospective inspection.

The objections I would expect from reviewers are these.

**A. The paper does not identify a ceiling.** The principal curve has not plateaued. More fundamentally, **exact subset-averaged union coverage has diminishing increments by construction**. Observing that shape is not itself a discovery about model limitations. The scientific questions are which errors remain detectable, what improves discrimination at comparable false-alarm rates, and why. Calling finite-budget union recall a “ceiling” creates expectations the study cannot meet.

**B. Most headline comparisons do not isolate the purported factor.** The general-code Claude routes receive temperature zero; the shipped GPT route receives no temperature parameter. I confirmed this in the provider code. Model, sampling variability, severity policy and sometimes call pathway change together. The manuscript admits this, but admitting a confound does not recover the inference. Neither the stronger-model comparison nor the cross-vendor comparison establishes comparative auditing ability or vendor independence.

**C. The effective sample sizes and scope are much smaller than the experimental programme suggests.**

- General-code defects: **110 instances from 56 problems**, generated by one model.
- Scientific-code defects: **81 instances from 30 problems**; **184/336 evaluated steps were excluded** because no generated sample passed.
- Scientific results: **74 underlying instances from 36 problems**.
- Scientific data: **four source datasets**, with repeated constructed samples.

The general-code tasks have median reference implementations of six lines. These are useful controlled experiments, but weak grounds for claims about automated auditing generally. SciCode also changes the information supplied and the defect-selection procedure, preventing a clean general-code versus scientific-code comparison.

**D. “Flagged defective code” is not “correctly diagnosed a defect”.** A model receives credit whenever it blocks a test-failing programme, even for an unrelated or erroneous objection. Conversely, a flag on test-passing code need not be false. The paper recognises this distinction, but several headline quantities remain poor proxies for the practical capability readers care about. Hidden tests are also hidden from the experimental prompt, not necessarily from model training.

**E. The specification explanation remains suggestive.** Its category combines three different situations: missing requirements, multiple permissible behaviours, and an oracle contradicting an otherwise determining specification. These require different explanations and remedies. Ratings are model-generated; one rater designed the programme.

The [clarification experiment](</Users/ericdong/Library/Application Support/Claude/scratch-workspaces/74ef214a-bb0b-45f3-a0dd-dab068ffcbe9/c2cff56f-cccf-49f5-bf0f-c2a959dccdde/scratch-2026-11-02-a4ee54/audit/audit-ceiling-paper/reports/RESULTS-CLARIFY.md>) does not resolve this: **23/32 additions contradicted the oracle**, and the nine diagnosed instances occupy only **five problems**. The manuscript’s exact cluster test gives \(p=0.0625\). Post hoc separation by whether the clarification was correct cannot establish the proposed mechanism.

**F. Scientific complementarity is promising but presently easy to obtain.** Provenance consistency checks necessarily miss consistently falsified artefacts. Clean results were selected to pass the deterministic checks, making their zero clean-flag rate partly a construction property. Re-execution is already a strong comparator, while the model saw deterministic findings in the results experiment.

For held-out data, I reproduced **90 → 105/140** lexical matches, with **9/140** clean flags. However:

- All 15 additions concern mixed units or swapped columns.
- All nine clean flags concern the airfoil card.
- The [scoring code](</Users/ericdong/Library/Application Support/Claude/scratch-workspaces/74ef214a-bb0b-45f3-a0dd-dab068ffcbe9/c2cff56f-cccf-49f5-bf0f-c2a959dccdde/scratch-2026-11-02-a4ee54/audit/audit-ceiling-paper/records/ai4s/analysis/diag-0981f67/diag_match.py>) checks names, not diagnostic correctness.
- These are new samples from the same tables. My exact-row comparison found that **99.2% of distinct held-out concrete clean rows and 94.2% of airfoil clean rows already occur in development clean files**. This does not invalidate the declared sampling experiment, but it sharply limits the meaning of “held-out”.
- The clean-flag interval reaches 10.5%; the realised rate satisfies the registered budget, but a population-level guarantee does not follow.

**G. The treatment of unsuccessful studies needs a methodological correction.** The [second-substrate report](</Users/ericdong/Library/Application Support/Claude/scratch-workspaces/74ef214a-bb0b-45f3-a0dd-dab068ffcbe9/c2cff56f-cccf-49f5-bf0f-c2a959dccdde/scratch-2026-11-02-a4ee54/audit/audit-ceiling-paper/reports/RESULTS-SUBSTRATE2.md:10>) describes its third run as technically valid, but withholds all manuscript numbers because only five of six expected reversals reproduced. **Failure of a substantive hypothesis is not grounds for suppressing technically valid measurements.** Recover and independently validate that run, then report its failure as a failure. Keep technically invalid runs separately identified.

**H. Too many studies dilute the contribution.** The general-code study map alone has 13 entries, followed by scientific-code arms, results, development data and held-out data. Several revisit the same small population. The PDF has eight main-text pages and 30 pages overall: the problem is density and fragmentation, rather than an obvious main-text page-count violation.

As area chair, I would regard the decisive issue as **insufficient scientific identification and generality**, rather than insufficient diligence. More review rounds or more qualifications would not resolve it.

**2. The strongest single paper**

I would focus the submission on **specification information and operating characteristics in code auditing**.

**Proposed title:**  
*What Limits LLM Code Auditing? Specifications, Decision Thresholds, and Verified Diagnosis*

**Central claim to establish:**  
*Providing the missing behavioural contract improves executable defect diagnosis at controlled false-alarm rates; the improvement cannot be explained solely by more aggressive flagging or repeated reading.*

That is a **target claim**, not a conclusion currently established. The existing material supports its motivation and some measurement machinery. The causal experiment below must carry the main scientific contribution.

An eight-page structure:

| Section | Pages | Purpose and evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Introduction | 0.75 | One concrete specification/oracle mismatch; one research question. |
| Formulation and related work | 0.75 | Separate benchmark disagreement, flagging and verified diagnosis. |
| Evaluation protocol | 1.25 | Ground truth, controls, task-level splits, budgets and estimands. |
| Operating characteristics | 1.50 | Existing repetition/rulebook results as motivation; new crossed-model curves as evidence. |
| Causal specification intervention | 1.75 | New randomised, oracle-consistent intervention with placebo and correct controls. |
| External validity | 1.00 | Newer tasks and a small repository-level replication. |
| Discussion and limitations | 0.75 | Information limitations, remaining confounds and practical interpretation. |
| Conclusion | 0.25 | Precisely bounded finding. |
| **Total** | **8.00** | References and appendix additional. |

Disposition of existing studies:

- **Carry the argument:** repeated reading; rulebook interventions; severity sweep, explicitly exploratory; residual analysis as hypothesis generation; clarification as a pilot explaining the redesigned experiment.
- **Appendix:** detailed route comparisons, revision outcomes, third-rater reliability, false-positive investigations, validated second-substrate results, complete registration/deviation table and robustness analyses.
- **Remove from this paper’s argument:** scientific results and data; failed injection construction; extended generated-test corroboration studies; review-round narratives and product receipt architecture. Preserve their records. The science material is a plausible separate project.

A paper centred only on “recall changes with the threshold” would remain too familiar. The specification intervention must establish something quantitatively stronger than “extra information sometimes helps”.

**3. Ranked experiments within ten weeks**

Use executable outcomes as primary endpoints. The author should validate task contracts **before seeing auditor responses**; any subsequent manual assessment should hide model identity and condition and remain secondary.

Across experiments, freeze prompts, model identifiers, CLI versions, tools, token/reasoning budgets, retries, exclusions and analysis before test calls. Split by original problem, repository or dataset, never by generated instance alone. Simulate power for the planned paired effect; treat the sample sizes below as feasible targets, not guarantees of precision.

For budgeting, current standard prices list GPT-6.1 Sol and Claude Sonnet 5.5 at approximately **$2/M input and $10/M output tokens**. An 8,000-input, 2,000-output response therefore costs about **$0.036** before retries and additional agent turns. The estimates below are API-equivalent expenditure, not promised CLI subscription charges. [OpenAI pricing](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing), [Anthropic pricing](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing).

| Rank | Experiment | Approximate calls | Planning cost |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| **1** | Causal specification intervention | 7,680 audit responses + up to 500 construction responses | **$250–450** |
| **2** | Crossed generators/auditors and operating curves on newer substrates | 12,000–13,200 responses | **$300–650** |

These are alternatives and extensions, not a recommendation to complete all four.

**Rank 1 — A genuine causal test of specification information**

- **Claim:** a correct missing requirement improves verified diagnosis beyond a threshold shift.
- **Substrates and N:** 240 distinct CPU-executable tasks, with a correct and defective candidate for each; 60 development and 180 test tasks. Include audited existing tasks and unpublished task variants. Keep natural failures and injected failures as separate strata.
- **Models:** GPT-6.1 Sol and Claude Sonnet 5.5 as auditors; candidates from both vendors.
- **Intervention:** hold code fixed and compare the underspecified contract, that contract plus a minimal correct requirement, and a length-matched irrelevant addition. Randomise execution order. Use two readings in every arm, plus two additional readings of the original arm.
- **Ground truth:** establish beforehand that the full contract and oracle agree, and that removing the clause admits distinguishable behaviours. Require executable counterexamples or equivalent checkable evidence from auditors. Do not provide the failing test input or its answer in the clarification.
- **Primary endpoint:** paired increase in verified-diagnosis recall at development-calibrated operating points, alongside the paired change in clean-item flagging. Freeze the acceptable false-alarm increase and minimum meaningful gain before testing.
- **Stronger result:** a substantial improvement across both auditors and both defect strata, surviving false-alarm control and exceeding the gain from additional original-arm readings.
- **Weaker result:** only more flags, only obvious injected bugs, or an effect disappearing against the placebo or at comparable false-alarm rates.

The most important design safeguard is **validating the intervention before auditing**. Another model’s declaration that a clarification is correct is insufficient.

**Rank 2 — Crossed models, fresh tasks and full operating characteristics**

- **Claim:** conclusions survive changes in generator and auditor, and represent discrimination rather than incompatible severity policies.
- **Substrates:** approximately 300 tasks divided between a recent, date-filtered [LiveCodeBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07974) release and a reproducibly executable [BigCodeBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.15877) subset. BigCodeBench adds complexity; it is not a contamination solution.
- **Models:** generators GPT-6.1 Sol and Claude Sonnet 5.5; auditors those two plus GPT-6.1 Terra and Claude Opus 5.5. Audit every retained candidate with every auditor.
- **N:** initially 1,200 generated candidates, targeting 300 visible-pass/hidden-fail and 300 test-passing audit items. Predeclare a generation cap and report a shortfall rather than selecting tasks until a desirable result appears. Reserve development problems separately.
- **Ground truth:** validated suites, reference checks and executable diagnostic witnesses. Add a matched-budget test-generation baseline; validate its asserted expectations against the evaluator’s oracle.
- **Primary endpoint:** partial ROC area over a prespecified low-FPR region, with verified recall at development-selected 5% and 10% budgets. Report held-out FPR intervals, PR curves with stated prevalence, and recall against tokens/dollars.
- **Preregistration:** collect a fixed confidence/severity score and \(K=4\) readings; freeze vote aggregation, calibration and primary contrasts. Use task-cluster uncertainty.
- **Stronger result:** the specification effect transfers, and improvements remain at comparable operating points.
- **Weaker result:** model rankings are almost entirely threshold effects, or conclusions depend on one generator.

**Equal temperature numbers do not imply equal sampling distributions.** Through the CLIs, match observable information and resource budgets, record supported settings, analyse \(K=1\) separately, and retain “configuration comparison” language wherever internal sampling remains uncontrolled.

For contamination, freeze model versions and task dates, disable browsing during evaluation, include unpublished semantic variants, and test transfer between original and variant tasks. Do not label the resulting evaluation “contamination-free”.

**Rank 3 — Demonstrate transfer beyond one product and tiny functions**

- **Claim:** the finding survives realistic code context and a simpler auditing interface.
- **Substrate and N:** 60 CPU-reproducible bug/fix pairs from roughly 10 Python repositories, giving 120 artefacts. Use a carefully screened SWE-bench-style subset or recent public fixes with reproducing tests. [SWE-bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06770) supplies the relevant methodological precedent.
- **Models/design:** Sol and Sonnet; CrossAudit prompt versus a minimal independent review prompt; four readings per artefact. Keep available code, requirements and tool permissions equal.
- **Ground truth:** regression tests that fail before and pass after the fix, plus scoped requirement checks. “Fixed” means the specified defect is resolved, not that the repository is defect-free.
- **Primary endpoint:** verified detection and clean-control flags at thresholds transferred without retuning; report results by repository.
- **Preregistration:** select cases by reproducibility and scope before model calls; freeze exclusions and evidence packaging.
- **Stronger result:** similar intervention effects under both wrappers and across projects.
- **Weaker result:** the effect disappears outside the shipped prompt, or findings mostly concern irrelevant files.

This is a bounded external-validity check. Sixty bugs do not establish general repository-auditing performance.

**Rank 4 — Scientific complementarity at meaningful scale**

This has high value for a **science-focused follow-up**, but lower value for the focused ICML paper above.

- **Claim:** model assistance adds verified detections to a strong deterministic baseline on unseen scientific sources, at a controlled false-alarm budget.
- **Substrates and N:** 12 datasets, four for development and eight entirely held out; 30 clean/corrupt pairs per dataset across 120, 1,200 and 12,000-row conditions. Add 60 CPU numerical workflows, each with one clean and three faulty result packages.
- **Models:** Sol and Sonnet, two audit sessions per item. Give bounded summaries and local Python access for large tables; record actual tool usage and cost.
- **Baselines:** comprehensive schema/range/unit/relational checks; reproducibility and re-execution checks; a model-generated validator frozen after development; model auditing; their union. Measure model auditing without exposing baseline findings first.
- **Ground truth:** injection manifests plus structured locations, fault types and executable numerical witnesses. For results, use independently computed answers and scientifically chosen tolerances. Column-name matching cannot be the primary endpoint.
- **Primary endpoint:** incremental verified recall over the strongest baseline at a development-selected clean-flag budget, with dataset/workflow-level uncertainty and cost.
- **Preregistration:** freeze fault families, realistic magnitudes, validators, dataset splits and scale conditions. Establish clean controls independently of whether the baseline accepts them.
- **Stronger result:** gains persist across unseen datasets, larger artefacts and several fault families.
- **Weaker result:** gains vanish after strengthening the validator, depend on one card, or require manual arithmetic on tiny examples.

**Execution schedule:** weeks 1–2, repair the evaluation definitions, validate contracts and preregister; weeks 3–5, experiments 1–2; week 6, evaluate the scientific result; weeks 7–8, a bounded transfer study if justified; weeks 9–10, analysis, writing and reproducibility checks. The core budget is approximately **$550–1,100**, before the optional transfer study.

Treat late January as a planning assumption: the [official ICML 2027 dates page](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/Dates) currently lists meeting dates but does not confirm that submission deadline. If the causal result is weak, use the additional time towards NeurIPS for substantive replication or the science study, rather than adding more small exploratory arms.

**5. Presentation changes that would materially help**

| Current example | Why it hurts | Concrete change |
|---|---|---|
| **“What Moves the Ceiling…”** | Promises a limit that is not identified. | Use “operating characteristics”, “verified diagnosis” or a question about limitations. |
| Abstract ends with **“every quoted report passed independent cross-vendor review”** | Sounds like external scientific validation, although the reviewers were models and sometimes measured subjects. | Remove from the abstract. Describe the checking procedure and its conflicts once in methods. |
| **“A model reviewing its own vendor’s output is an interested reader.”** | Anthropomorphic and unsupported as a causal explanation. Vendor difference does not establish independent errors. | State an empirical hypothesis about correlated errors and test it with crossed generators and auditors. |
| **“A check’s finding is verified: code produced it and code can reproduce it.”** | Reproducibility does not establish that the rule encodes the right requirement. | “The finding is reproducible under the specified executable check.” |
| **“Ground truth no model supplies.”** | Too broad when important diagnosis and specification claims depend on model adjudication. | Name the source separately: execution labels, injection manifests, or model ratings. |
| Main Table 1 combines roughly twenty quantities from different populations and protocols. | Readers cannot identify the three results carrying the contribution. | Replace with a compact primary-result table; move the study inventory and ancillary contrasts to the appendix. |
| **“Nine further studies, and what they license.”** | Makes the paper read like a laboratory chronicle. | Organise by scientific question; keep study IDs as provenance metadata. |
| Repeated **“quotable”, “killed”, review-round counts and repair histories** | Internal governance vocabulary consumes attention without establishing validity. | Use “primary criterion not met”, “exploratory” and “excluded because…”. Consolidate deviations into one table. |
| **“Which we did not find in prior work.”** | A search outcome is not a demonstrated novelty boundary. | Compare methods explicitly on substrates, ground truth, operating-point control and causal intervention. |

The opening snake-case example is effective and should stay. Follow it immediately with the precise distinction between **a programme violating its contract** and **a programme disagreeing with a hidden oracle**. That distinction should organise the paper, its endpoints and its claims.
